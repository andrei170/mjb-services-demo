Straight answers

What operators ask us

Including the one that decides whether a machine is worth fixing.

How often should a commercial ice machine be cleaned?

For most Florida kitchens, every three to six months — and quarterly if it is high volume. Our climate and our water are both hard on these machines. If yours has never been deep cleaned and it is more than a year old, that is the first thing to book.

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

We will tell you honestly. There is a point on most commercial equipment where the next repair is the third one this year and replacing is simply cheaper. Firms that only sell repairs have a reason not to say that. We would rather keep the account for the next ten years.

Do you work on residential as well as commercial?

Yes. Commercial refrigeration and ice machines are the specialism, but we do residential AC across Tampa Bay too.

What does a maintenance plan actually cover?

Scheduled visits through the year, cleaning and checks on the equipment you nominate, a written record of each visit, and priority if something does go down. The exact schedule depends on your equipment and how hard it works — a busy bar's ice machine needs more attention than an office fridge.
